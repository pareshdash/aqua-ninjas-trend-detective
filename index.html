2. Why Now? The Texas City-to-Gulf Runoff Pathway

NASA satellites cannot directly see microplastics inside a neighborhood creek. Instead, Trend Detective tests the upstream extreme-rainfall driver that pushes street oil and plastic into local waterways:

1

Extreme Rain

Intense rainstorms (>50 mm/day) generate sudden urban stormwater runoff.

2

Urban Runoff

Runoff washes waste oil, grease, and broken-down plastic fragments off streets into storm drains.

3

Creeks & River Basin

Storm drains empty untreated into local creeks and downstream Texas rivers and bays.

4

Gulf of Mexico

Watershed outflows carry persistent plastics and surfactants downstream toward the Gulf.
